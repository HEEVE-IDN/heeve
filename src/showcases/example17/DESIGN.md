# Example 17 — Plainform

DesignMD source: the supplied Plainform brief informed this implementation.

Warm editorial home-goods storefront. Cream paper surfaces, terracotta action color, oversized typography, generous whitespace, quiet product photography. Dark mode swaps to espresso surfaces with warm ivory text and lighter terracotta accent. Product content stays in `config.json`; shared `ShowcaseCatalog` and `ProductGallery` handle commerce interactions.
