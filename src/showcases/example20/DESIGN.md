## Direction

A human-first collaboration storefront: quiet blue surfaces, generous editorial spacing, candid team photography, crisp utility navigation, and small direct calls to action. The page should feel like a calm meeting room rather than a software dashboard.

## System

- Light: near-white canvas, pale blue surface, navy ink, blue action.
- Dark: deep navy canvas, blue-black surface, pale ink, sky-blue action.
- Editorial hero with image, angled annotation, and oversized display type.
- Shared `ShowcaseCatalog` for filtering/cards; shared `ProductGallery` for detail pages.
- CSS-only layout; no animation dependency beyond existing `AnimatedContent`.
